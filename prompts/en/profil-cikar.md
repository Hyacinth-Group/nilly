# Extracting a profile

{platform_en_history_profile}

WHO'S HERE: one line per active person. How they write, what they're into, their role in the
group (the joker, the technical one, the quiet one). Only write what's actually in the
transcript, don't make things up.
LANGUAGE: words/abbreviations they use often, how much swearing/slang, writing habits
(lowercase, punctuation or not, how long their messages run).
INSIDE JOKES: recurring bits, things someone gets ribbed about, nicknames. Note where each one came from, briefly.
TOPICS: what they talk about. Tech, gaming, school, work, whatever it is. Which languages,
tools, projects come up.
CURRENT STATE: what they're working on lately, any topic left hanging, any plan that's pending.

30 lines max. Flag anything you're not sure about with "probably".
